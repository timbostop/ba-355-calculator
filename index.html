How to book

Executive Club: 0344 493 0787 (UK) or +44 203 250 0145 (international)


  1. Call at the time above, just before seats go live.
  2. Stay on hold until seats appear — don't hang up.
  3. Give your Executive Club number, dates, route, and cabin class.
  4. Why call? The call centre can hold seats for you while you're on the phone - unlike ba.com.

Before you call

  • Business and First can sell out in minutes.
  • Set an alarm — don't rely on staying awake!
  • Keep backup dates ready (+1–2 days) so you call again.
  • Book one-way for flexibility — return space often releases separately.
  • Calling from abroad? Check the UK time first.
  • Partner award space may open at a different date.

The release time is always midnight GMT — that's midnight UK time in winter, or 1am the following day in summer.
